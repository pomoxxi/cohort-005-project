# Cadence

Cadence is a course platform where learners buy courses and watch their lessons. This glossary fixes the words for a learner getting their money back.

## Language

### Refund Requests

**Refund Request**:
A learner asking for their money back for a course they bought. A team member decides it. A learner can open a new one after a decline.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback, dispute

**Open**:
A Refund Request that the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Requested, pending

**Approved**:
A Refund Request that the team accepted. It leads to a Refund.

**Declined**:
A Refund Request that the team turned down. It leads to no Refund.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

### Refunds

**Refund**:
The money going back to the learner after their Refund Request is Approved. It is always the full amount the learner paid.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund that has been approved but not yet confirmed as sent.

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund that couldn't be sent. Its Refund Request stays Approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
