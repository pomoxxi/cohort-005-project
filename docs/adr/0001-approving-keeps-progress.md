# Approving a Refund Request keeps the learner's progress

When a Platform Admin approves a Refund Request, the learner loses their place on the course but keeps their lesson progress, watch history, quiz attempts, comments and rating, including that rating's place in the course average. Deleting can't be undone, the watch history is the evidence the approval was decided on, and a learner who comes back should pick up where they left off. Progress rows for a learner with no place on the course are expected. They aren't orphans to clean up.
