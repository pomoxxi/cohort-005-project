# Approving a Refund Request keeps the Student's progress

When a Platform Admin approves a Refund Request on an Individual Purchase, the buyer loses their Enrollment but keeps their lesson progress, watch history, quiz attempts, comments and rating. Deleting it can't be undone, the watch history is the evidence the approval was decided on, and a Student who buys again or claims a Seat later should pick up where they left off. Rows for a Student with no Enrollment are expected, not orphans to clean up.
