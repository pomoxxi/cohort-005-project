# A learner gets at most one Refund per course

A Refund Request is refused when the buyer already has an Approved Refund Request on an earlier Purchase of the same course. They can still buy the course again, or claim a Seat, but they can't get a second Refund. Without this rule, a learner could keep buying, watching up to the limit and getting refunded, and watch the whole course for free. A Declined Refund Request doesn't count. There's no Platform Admin override.
